**This is a Markdown file containing the basic information about the Moorea Coral Reef Model we are creating in EDS221**

The Moorea Coral Reef is located on and around the island of Tahiti. Although it is a very resilient ecosystem, there are several disturbances that threaten the health, persistence, and biomass of the coral ecosystem. The coral reef is predominantly made up of three types of coral genus: Acropora, Pocillopora, and Porites. To model coral growth, our simulation uses a dice to determine whether or not the coral will grow or die, based on relative probabilities. However, there are 3 disturbances that occur in between our time span from 2004 to 2024. These disturbances, that threaten the coral reef's health, are the growth of invasive predators (crown-of-thorns sea stars), cyclones, and marine heat waves.
